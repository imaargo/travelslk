Who we are

Local knowledge, proper planning

TravelsLK was built around a simple idea: Sri Lanka is best seen with people who actually live here. We're a small team of guides, planners and water people who know which beach is quiet at 4pm and which boat handles the swell best.

We plan tailor-made tours, yacht days, helicopter flights, MICE programmes and activity days — and we look after the transport, timing and small details in between. One conversation, one plan, one team.
