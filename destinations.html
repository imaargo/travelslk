Where the island slows down

South coast, at your pace

Start in Galle's old fort, drift through Hikkaduwa's reefs, learn to surf in Weligama and wake early for whales in Mirissa. End with long, quiet beaches around Tangalle.

  • Whale watching & dolphin tours from Mirissa
  • Sunset boat tours and yacht days
  • Surfing, snorkelling and turtle spotting
  • Galle Fort walks and coastal train rides

Temples, caves and ancient capitals

The Cultural Triangle holds Sri Lanka's most important heritage sites. Go early, take a guide and give yourself time to sit with it.

  • Sigiriya rock fortress at sunrise
  • Dambulla cave temple
  • Anuradhapura & Polonnaruwa ancient cities
  • Village lunches and spice gardens
