Questions, answered

Everything travellers usually ask before booking. If yours isn't here, message us — we're quick on WhatsApp.

How do I book an experience?
Use the Plan Your Journey form or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us the experience, dates and group size; we'll confirm availability, the final quote and the next steps. No payment is taken through the form.
Do I need to pay a deposit?
Some experiences require a deposit to hold a boat, helicopter or guide; others are confirmed on the day. We'll always tell you the terms in writing before you pay anything.
What currencies and payment methods do you accept?
Prices are quoted in USD, with LKR and EUR shown approximately on the site. Depending on the experience we can arrange card payment, bank transfer, PayPal or KOKO. Final payment details are confirmed with your booking.
Are the activities suitable for children?
Many are — whale watching, calm-water boat tours and turtle snorkelling are popular with families. Some water sports have age or height requirements, and children under 5 often travel free on select experiences. Tell us ages and we'll advise honestly.
What happens if the weather is bad?
Water activities depend on sea and weather conditions. If we can't run safely, we'll offer to move your date or suggest a land alternative. Any refund or reschedule is handled under the experience's terms, which we share before booking.
Can you arrange airport transfers and hotels?
Yes — hotel booking and airport transfers are part of our services, along with island-wide transport in cars, vans, SUVs and coaches. Add them to your itinerary or ask for a transport quote.
Do you help with visas and air tickets?
We can point you to the official visa resources and help coordinate air ticketing as part of a package. Visa approval always rests with the Sri Lankan authorities.
Can you build a full tailor-made itinerary?
Absolutely — that's our favourite kind of work. Share your dates, budget, travel style and must-dos, and we'll design a route with timings and a clear quote. Adjust it as many times as you like.
Are your guides English-speaking?
Our guides speak English, and we can arrange other languages on request depending on availability. Let us know in advance and we'll do our best.
How far in advance should I book?
For boats, helicopters and peak-season dates, earlier is better — a few days to a few weeks ahead. For simple activity days, we can often arrange things at short notice. Message us and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.
