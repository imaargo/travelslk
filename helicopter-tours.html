Before you fly

Good to know

Where do flights depart from?
The Colombo Sky tour departs from the Waters Edge Hotel heli pad in Battaramulla. Other routes can be arranged on request.
How many people fit?
The Colombo Sky scenic ride takes up to 6 passengers per flight. Larger groups can be split across multiple flights.
Can we book for a wedding or proposal?
Yes — we arrange helicopter pre-shoots, proposals and celebrations with photography, videography, drone and decoration add-ons.
