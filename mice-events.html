What is MICE?

Business travel that people actually remember

MICE stands for Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions — the four pillars of corporate and group travel. In Sri Lanka it can mean a boardroom in Colombo, a team retreat in the tea hills, a conference by the sea or a product launch on a yacht.

We handle the pieces that make it work: transport, accommodation, venues, activities, catering, photography and the small surprises that turn a schedule into an experience.
